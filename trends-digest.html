PRODUCT NOTES

Spot The Choices That Bring A Product Together.

Good references do more than set a mood. They help clarify a silhouette, surface, color relationship or detail that can be carried into a specification.

This edit looks at four useful prompts: garment character, texture, headwear and packaging. Use what fits your project, then confirm the choices against your own brief.

NOTES FOR YOUR NEXT PROJECT

Signals Shaping The Brief

These are creative prompts, not market forecasts. Adapt what is useful to your product, audience and production requirements.

APPAREL / IDENTITY

Build A Club Capsule Around One Anchor.

Choose one garment or graphic to carry the strongest identity, then let the other pieces support it. A shared color story, consistent placement and a considered size range can make a small assortment feel connected.

Put In The Brief

List each garment, decoration location, artwork version, size and color mix. If a mark appears on more than one item, note whether its dimensions change.

MATERIAL / FINISH

Let The Surface Do Some Of The Work.

When a product already has a noticeable texture, consider how decoration will sit against it. Contrast can add clarity; a quieter treatment can leave the material in focus.

Put In The Brief

Share a close reference for the surface, finished artwork size, color direction and any fine lines or lettering that need review.

HEADWEAR / PLACEMENT

Design Around The Panel.

A cap front is curved and divided by seams. Panel shape, profile and decoration placement affect how a graphic reads at its finished size.

Put In The Brief

Include the cap profile, closure preference, artwork size and approximate placement. A mockup can help compare the mark with seams and curves.

PRESENTATION / PACKAGING

Think About First Look And Handoff.

Labels and packaging affect how a product is stored, shipped, grouped or presented. Consider these details alongside the item itself.

Put In The Brief

Note the packing unit, labels, inserts, protection and delivery destination requirements you already know.

KEEP THE IDEAS CLOSE

Save The Notes And Shape A Product Brief.
