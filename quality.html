QUALITY & PROJECT SUPPORT

Quality starts with the specification.

Agree what to inspect, keep an approved sample and document the checks required before dispatch.

Discuss your project →

Inspection scope and terms are agreed with your quotation.

Six stages of quality control.

01Specification review

Record composition, weight, color references, measurements, artwork dimensions and intended use. Flag destination-specific requirements early.

02Sample approval

Review the physical sample or agreed proof. Record revisions and keep the approved version as the production reference.

03Production follow-up

Track production against the approved specification. Record revisions and resolve material or decoration questions before proceeding.

04In-line inspection

Agree checks during production to identify stitching, placement or measurement issues before the full run is complete.

05Final inspection

Review measurements, stitching, decoration placement and appearance against the agreed tolerances. Discuss testing or independent inspection when needed.

06Packing & dispatch

Check the quantity, size breakdown, labels and carton marks. Agree the dispatch documents and delivery arrangements before release.

Documentation that matches your product.

Support after delivery.

Share an issue

Send the order reference, affected quantity and clear photographs. Keep the packaging and a representative sample while the issue is reviewed.

Review the next step

Compare the issue with the approved specification and agree an appropriate next step, such as replacement, rework or credit, under the order terms.
