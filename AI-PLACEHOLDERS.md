## New image prompts (built-in imagegen)

### Embroidery detail
One landscape 3:2 photorealistic macro photograph for an apparel embroidery technique section on a premium fashion sourcing demo website. Tight close-up of an unbranded small navy geometric sun emblem embroidered directly onto cream cotton sweatshirt jersey. Visible individual satin stitches following the radial shape, believable slight raised thread relief, fine cotton knit texture, subtle natural folds. The embroidery occupies roughly half the frame so thread quality can be studied. Soft daylight, restrained editorial styling, realistic matte fabric, navy and cream palette. No patch border, no separate badge backing: embroidery directly into garment. No text, letters, logos, watermark, hands, machines or props. No plastic texture or exaggerated depth of field. A concept finish sample, not a claimed customer order.

### Sample review
Create one photorealistic editorial photograph for a premium apparel sourcing demo website, landscape 3:2 composition. A sample review tabletop, close view of only one person's natural hands and forearms comparing a small plain navy fabric swatch against a cream cotton sweatshirt. Several neatly arranged blue and cream thread swatches and a simple unbranded geometric embroidered patch sit nearby. Quiet modern fashion product development mood, natural window light, authentic woven textile texture, restrained cream and muted blue palette. Focus on the physical review action and materials; no face, no identity, no logos, no letters, no numbers, no readable writing, no certificates, no laptop screen, no machinery. Anatomically plausible hands, understated realistic photography, not glossy stock imagery. Image is a conceptual illustration of sample review, not evidence of a real company or employee.
