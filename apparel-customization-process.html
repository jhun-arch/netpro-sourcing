APPAREL CUSTOMIZATION / PROJECT FLOW

A Clear Process For Custom Apparel

Bring the product idea, artwork and requirements you have. We will work through the open questions with you and confirm the project scope before anything moves into production.

Share A Project Brief Review The Five Steps

FROM FIRST NOTE TO DISPATCH

Five Steps Keep The Specification Clear

Each project has its own requirements. These steps show the information we work through together; timing, sample scope and sourcing details are confirmed in the quotation.

01

Brief

Share the garment or product, estimated quantity, artwork, placement, size range and delivery destination.

02

Artwork Review

We review artwork scale, colors, decoration approach and garment details, then raise any questions that need a decision.

03

Sample

If a sample or proof is included in the agreed scope, review the garment, placement and finish against the brief.

04

Production Agreement

Confirm the written quote, specifications, quantity, packing, destination and agreed project terms before proceeding.

05

Delivery

We coordinate packing and dispatch according to the agreed destination and terms, and share available shipment details.

WHAT TO PREPARE

A Useful First Brief Can Be Simple

Send the information you already have. We can clarify the remaining details during review.

  • Product style, fabric and garment colors
  • Artwork file or a clear visual reference
  • Decoration type and intended placement
  • Estimated quantity and size breakdown
  • Delivery destination and target date, if known

BEFORE A RUN IS CONFIRMED

Agree The Details That Define The Order

Before production begins, confirm the approved artwork, garment, colors, placement, quantity, packing, destination, quotation and applicable terms in writing. If a sample is part of the scope, use the agreed reference to resolve changes first.

When requirements change after approval, pause to review the effect on specification and quotation before proceeding.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Process Questions

What Should I Send First?

A product reference, artwork or visual example, estimated quantity and destination are a useful start. Include any known size range, placement or material requirements.

Does Every Project Include A Sample?

Sample or proof requirements depend on the project and are confirmed with the quotation. We will explain what is included before you approve the scope.

When Is Production Confirmed?

After the quote and written specification are reviewed and accepted. The project terms will describe the agreed next steps.

How Is Delivery Planned?

Destination, packing and delivery arrangements are discussed as part of the quotation. Any schedule or shipment details are confirmed for that project.
