Frequently asked questions

Can I buy without customization?

Add standard styles to your enquiry. We confirm availability, specifications and pricing before you order.

How do I request custom or bulk pricing?

Choose Add to Enquiry, add reference styles to your enquiry list and share your project requirements. You can also enquire without selecting a product.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on product and technique. Include your target quantity so the applicable minimum can be confirmed. The quantity selector is not an MOQ guarantee.

Can I request a sample?

Ask for a sample or artwork proof in your brief. Availability, cost, revision scope and timing are agreed for each project.

Which artwork should I provide?

Upload JPG, PNG, PDF or ZIP files with your quote enquiry (up to 3 files, 2 MB total), or share an accessible file link. Include dimensions, placement and color references. Vector artwork is useful when available; a clear reference image can start the discussion.

How do I choose materials and sizes?

Confirm fiber composition, fabric weight, measurements and tolerances for the selected product. Images and selectable sizes do not replace an approved specification sheet.

How long does delivery take?

Provide the destination and required arrival date. Sampling, approvals, production and shipping need separate allowance. The schedule is confirmed with your quotation.

What about returns or product issues?

Confirm return arrangements for standard products before purchase. Agree custom specifications and issue-resolution terms before approval. Contact us with the order reference and photographs if a concern arises.

Can you meet a testing or environmental standard?

State the exact standard and documentation required. Certifications, material content and testing scope must be verified for the specific product; no blanket certification is claimed.

Does the form send automatically?

Yes. Submit the form to send your requirements to our team. The status message confirms success or explains how to retry.
